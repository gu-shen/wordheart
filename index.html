Wordheart

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Wordheart ("the App", "we", "us") is an AI-powered greeting card and message writing app. This policy explains what data the App collects, why, and how it is handled. The short version: we collect as little as possible, we never sell your data, and we never see your payment details.

The one thing you should know: the notes you type are sent to an AI service (Moonshot AI) to generate your message, and that processing happens on servers located in China. Your notes are never used to train AI models and are never shared with anyone else. Details below.

1. Information the App collects

  • Your notes and occasion selection. The text you type on the writing screen and the occasion you pick (birthday, apology, etc.). This is sent to our server to generate your message.
  • Device identifier. A random UUID generated on first launch and stored in your device's secure keychain. It is used to enforce the free daily limit and to link your subscription to your device. It is not an advertising identifier and contains no personal information.
  • Subscription status. When you subscribe, the App Store provides a cryptographically signed purchase receipt, which the App forwards to our server to unlock premium features. We never see your name, email, or payment details — Apple processes all payments.
  • Feedback you send. If you use the in-app feedback form, your message, optional email, app version, and selected occasion are stored so we can read and reply to your feedback.
  • Generated messages. The messages the AI writes for you are briefly cached on our server so that repeated requests for the same notes return instantly.

2. How your data is processed

When you tap "Write", your notes are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our backend, which runs on Cloudflare's global network. The backend forwards your notes to Moonshot AI (Beijing Moonshot Technology Co., Ltd., China) to generate your message.

  • Your notes are used only to generate your message in that moment. They are not used to train AI models.
  • Your notes and generated messages are cached on our server for up to 30 days, so identical notes can be answered instantly without another AI call. The cache is keyed by a one-way hash — it is not stored alongside your device identifier.
  • Daily-limit records (a flag per device per day) are kept for 2 days. Subscription records are kept until your subscription ends. Debugging logs are deleted after 7 days. Feedback is kept until it is no longer needed.

3. What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use tracking SDKs or analytics tools that follow you across apps and websites.
  • We do not see or store your Apple ID, payment card, or billing address — purchases are handled entirely by Apple.
  • We do not link your device identifier to your identity.

4. Data sharing

Data is processed by the following services, strictly to operate the App:

  • Moonshot AI (China) — generates your messages from your notes.
  • Cloudflare — hosts our backend, cache, and encrypted transport.
  • Apple — processes subscriptions and provides purchase receipts.
  • Resend — delivers feedback emails you send us to our inbox.

Each provider receives only the minimum data needed for its role.

5. Your rights

You can delete everything we hold about your device at any time: in the App, remove your data by reinstalling is not required — because your device identifier lives in the secure keychain, simply contact us below with your Device ID (shown in Settings) and we will delete your quota, subscription, and cached records within 7 days.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to access, correct, export, or erase your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Contact us to exercise any of these rights.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area and United Kingdom) and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

All network traffic between the App and our backend is encrypted with TLS. Purchase receipts are verified cryptographically (Apple's X.509 chain) before any subscription is honored. Access to server data is restricted to what is needed to operate the service.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. For significant changes, we will also note the change inside the App.
